Blueprint1989 is an expense-sharing platform designed to help groups track shared costs, split bills, and manage financial obligations with complete transparency.

Whether managing group bills, shared subscriptions, or other group expenses, Blueprint1989 gives every member a clear view of who owes what and when.

Expenses are organised into cycles, creating structured billing periods that make it easy to review, settle, and close out group finances at a glance.

Blueprint1989 supports real-time push notifications, offline entry, biometric login, and a full payment confirmation workflow so groups can stay on top of their finances from any device.
