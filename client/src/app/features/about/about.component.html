Vitara is a health management platform designed to equip individuals and families to confidently manage ongoing health conditions.

The name Vitara was derived from vita,the Latin word for meaning life,ara, inspired by Arya the daughter of the founder who was diagnosed with Type 1 Diabetes at six years old.

What began as a personal need for clearer tracking and better organisation evolved into a structured system built to empower, independence, clarity and self‑management.

Vitara currently supports the management of Diabetes and High Blood Pressure and is intentionally designed to expand into broader health monitoring over time.

Vitara supports both assisted care and independent use, empowering people of all ages to understand, monitor and manage their health with confidence,even in the absence of immediate support.

Inspired by Arya.
